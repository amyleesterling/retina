The cast of the retina

One proofread cell of each of 117 cell types in the Eyewire II retina, reconstructed from electron microscope images of mouse retina. Each tile is drawn at its own scale, given by its bar. Open any cell to turn it and zoom in.

Types are the labels people gave these cells in the main proofreading list. A type is here if at least 10 proofread cells carry its label. Each example is a cell marked complete whose branches stay inside the volume; 12 more types had no such cell among their first 15 candidates, so they are left out rather than shown cut off. Meshes are simplified for the browser, so the finest branches can look beaded.
